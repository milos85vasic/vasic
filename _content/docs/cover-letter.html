Cover Letter

Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to express my interest in engineering roles focused on building production AI systems. I am an AI engineer who builds the unglamorous, load-bearing part of AI software: the LLM infrastructure, the autonomous agents and orchestration, and the QA and governance layers that make them trustworthy in production.

Over the past years I have designed and shipped an interconnected family of AI-development products. The Helix line spans the full lifecycle — HelixAgent (an ensemble LLM service where multiple models debate and ship the answer they agree on), HelixCode (a distributed AI development platform that divides work across SSH-managed workers with checkpoint/rollback), HelixLLM (a single binary serving OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible inference over HTTP/3), and the LLM-infrastructure trio LLMProvider, LLMOrchestrator, and LLMsVerifier (one interface over 43 providers, a control plane for headless CLI agents, and a verification source of truth). Around these I built product-grade tools such as Catalogizer (multi-protocol, encrypted media management) and Courses-Creator (an AI markdown-to-video course pipeline), all standing on a fleet of small, decoupled, independently-tested Go and Kotlin Multiplatform modules.

What I think sets my work apart is a discipline I take seriously: anti-bluff engineering. I maintain a universal engineering Constitution, distributed as a Git submodule and inherited across a 140+-repository fleet, that makes one rule mechanically enforceable — a feature is not done when the tests pass, it is done when a real user can use it and there is captured evidence to prove it. I pair that with HelixQA, an anti-bluff QA orchestrator that runs autonomous LLM-and-computer-vision sessions across Android, Web, and Desktop and refuses to score a PASS without a screenshot, a logcat, or a video. I build systems that don’t just look finished; they demonstrably are.

Technically I work primarily in Go, with Kotlin/KMP, TypeScript/React, Python, Swift, and Shell, across REST/gRPC/HTTP/3 services, PostgreSQL/SQLite/Redis/ClickHouse data layers, and Docker/Kubernetes/Prometheus operations. I am comfortable owning a system from provider abstraction and retrieval all the way to the evidence trail that proves it works.

I would welcome the chance to bring that combination — deep AI-systems engineering plus a genuine, verifiable quality discipline — to your team. Thank you for your consideration; my portfolio and public repositories are available at milosvasic.ru and vasic.digital.
