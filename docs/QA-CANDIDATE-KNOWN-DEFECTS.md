### Method notes and limits

- Candidate checks used `git -C <candidate> grep/show/log`; the Go, Karma and shell tests were not run for this digest.
- 'OPEN' means the code or text the report criticised is still present in the candidate tree at HEAD. 'LIKELY-FIXED-LATER' means a later commit on the merged branch tip names the finding and the changed code looks like the remedy, without a fresh independent review. 'DORMANT' means present but unreachable or off by default. 'UNKNOWN' means not determined.
- The candidate-introduced compose regression (ApiKey_ lines) is not in any review; it appears because the candidate merges both the earlier apikeys-wiring design and its supersession.
- completion-backlog A and B are audits of 2026-10-21 against int-0.0.3 and main; many of their items (practice hand-over, event source, crossref floor, FB2/DOCX/HTML extractors, scoring adapters) were completed by the n-streams and are omitted here after checking. Only items still observable in the candidate were kept.
