## Defect class

The stale-figures detector had a false negative (the "<n> declared" / "<n> owned" figure shapes, e.g. CLAUDE.md
saying 14 declared while `.gitmodules` declares more) and two false-positive families (a withdrawal stated on the
NEXT line; exit-code MEANING lines such as "exit 0 = clean").

## Measurable target

"corpus gains >=3 planted rows (declared/owned shapes) detected and >=2 clean controls not reported; live
figure:CLAUDE.md:315 reported for the 14/22 mismatch". The probe builds its own throwaway repos (it reads no
fixtures from the tree) and the live shape from the pre-fix CLAUDE.md, so editing a fixture cannot satisfy it.
It measures exactly the three clauses. RED on the pre-fix ref (outcome 1), GREEN on the working tree (outcome 0).

## Alternatives considered

1. Add the shapes only: leaves the two FP families, the stated precision stays wrong.
2. Suppress whole documents: hides real drift (rejected).
3. Narrow, line-anchored rules (chosen): next-line marker only when the next line opens with a deictic subject and
   names the marker in its first sentence; exit-code meaning only when every figure on the line is an exit shape
   followed by "=", an arrow or "means", and no non-exit figure shares the line.

## Why the fix is right

Each rule has a mutation in `--prove-failure` (M10, M11, M1f) showing the control is quiet because of the rule:
remove the withdrawal line or turn the meaning line into a measured status and the finding returns.

## Recall cost (stated honestly)

A genuinely stale figure whose NEXT line begins "That/This/It ... withdrawn|superseded|historical|obsolete" in its
first sentence is now invisible, as is a line whose only figures are exit-code meanings. The rules are deliberately
narrow (a bare marker elsewhere on the next line does not count; a measured status like "exits 0 at 12 CURRENT" is
still a figure). Whether the narrowing hides a real defect elsewhere in the tree was not exhaustively measured.
