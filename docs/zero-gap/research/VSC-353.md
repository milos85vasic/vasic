## What went wrong the first time

The first fix (earlier fixer, earlier check) added "declared"/"owned" to the figure grammar and two false-positive
skips. The review chain withheld it: it made the "14 declared" line show up as a generic "no re-measure command"
finding, a relabelling of the old unanchored 0-DRIFT report. It never compared the claim with reality (the
`.gitmodules` path count), so the actual defect (a stated 14 against 22 real declarations) was still not detected as a
mismatch. My own earlier note and diary row (id 24) accepted that and are superseded; the old sealed records stay as
history.

## What changed

- A new check author rewrote the check with four clauses: (A) at least 3 planted declared/owned rows detected and 2
  clean controls (next-line withdrawal, exit-code meaning line) quiet; (B) a real mismatch: a sandbox `.gitmodules`
  declaring 22 paths and a current-tense "14 declared" line must yield a finding that names BOTH figures and says
  mismatch; (C) equal counts (22 vs 22) are not a mismatch; (D) a `(historical)`-marked line is not a mismatch.
- The class now reads the number of `path =` lines of the `.gitmodules` at `--root` and compares every "<n> declared"
  on a surviving figure line with it. Commit 9ad3985 narrowed this to a fleet noun (submodule, gitlink, module, repo,
  repository, path, "in total", "in all") or end of sentence, because "9 declared pairs" and "76 declared members"
  were live false positives. The `--prove-failure` battery has G1-G10 (including mutants that skip the comparison or
  compare against the wrong count).

## Measurable target

"corpus gains >=3 planted rows (declared/owned shapes) detected and >=2 clean controls not reported; live
figure:CLAUDE.md:315 reported for the 14/22 mismatch". The detector now reports a genuine disagreement between a
claimed count and the real count, so the target is met by detection and not by relabelling. The live line 315 is no
longer the evidence: commit 7691790 marked the two stale CONTINUATION readings historical, and the probe measures
the mismatch in its own sandbox rather than in a carrier.

## Alternatives considered

1. Keep the generic "no re-measure command" label: relabelling, rejected by review.
2. Compare against the owned-submodule count too: there is no honest source for it in a bare repository, so the check
   deliberately has no owned clause.
3. Compare "declared <any noun>": produced false positives on pairs/members; narrowed to fleet nouns (chosen).

## Recall cost (stated honestly)

- A real fleet claim phrased "<n> declared <other noun>" (for example "13 declared entries" or "13 declared owned"), or
  whose noun wraps onto the next line, is not compared and is invisible as a mismatch.
- A stale figure that sits only on a line marked historical, withdrawn or superseded, or on a line followed by a
  deictic "That figure is WITHDRAWN" line, is invisible by design; so is a line made only of exit-code meanings.
- The "owned" figures are detected as figures but never compared with a real count.
- The comparison needs a `.gitmodules` at `--root`; without one it is skipped with a NOTE on stderr, not a finding.
