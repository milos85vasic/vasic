// OVERVIEW
One fleet, one discipline
One fleet: large product applications on top of dozens of small, decoupled, independently-
tested modules — governed by a shared engineering Constitution and verified by anti-bluff
QA. The dominant language is Go, with Kotlin/KMP, TypeScript/React, Python, Swift, and
Shell where they fit best. The unifying thesis: a feature is done only when a real user can use
it and there is captured evidence to prove it.
