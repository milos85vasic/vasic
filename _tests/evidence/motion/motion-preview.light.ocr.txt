Animation & interaction module
Every effect below is driven by OpenDesign motion tokens (--od-dur-*,
--od-ease-*) and animates only transform and opacity for smooth,
compositor-friendly motion.

Reduced motion: this page fully respects prefers-reduced-motion: reduce. With it enabled, all

reveals, staggers, bounces, blinks, hovers, the accordion height animation, view transitions and the Lottie

accents are neutralized — content appears instantly in its final state, with no looping motion.
