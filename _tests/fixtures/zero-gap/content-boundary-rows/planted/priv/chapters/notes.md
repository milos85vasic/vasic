# Lantern survey notes

## Lanterns guide the river boats home

The harbour keeper explained that the green lanterns along the eastern quay are lit an hour before dusk so that the river boats can find the channel even when the fog rolls in from the marsh.

## Quiet mill wheels turn slowly at dawn

Every morning the old mill wheel turns slowly while the bakers wait for the first sacks of flour to arrive from the upper valley farms.

Attendance: Quorvel Tansiby recorded the lantern count for the survey.
